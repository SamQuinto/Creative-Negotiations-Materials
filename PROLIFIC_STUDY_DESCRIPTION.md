# Prolific study description

In this study, you will take part in an interactive job-offer negotiation. You will be assigned a role, receive information about your objectives and priorities, and negotiate with another participant over video using our online interface.

If you reach an agreement, each point you earn is worth **US$1, up to a maximum negotiation bonus of US$15**. Without an agreement, you receive no negotiation bonus. Your base payment is unaffected.

Afterwards, you will complete a questionnaire about your experience. Participants who pass our survey-effort checks will receive a **separate bonus of 10–50% of their base payment**, based on the care and quality of their responses.
